---
name: Official logo sourcing
description: Source-verification constraint when refreshing the HR clients' letterhead logos.
---

Corporate websites can block raw HTTP requests even when their rendered pages and public media assets are accessible. Do not treat a bot-challenge response as evidence that the brand has no available official logo.

**Why:** Riyadh Air and Red Sea Global returned challenge/error pages to raw page fetches, while rendered branding extraction identified their accessible original header logos. Image search also returned unrelated subsidiary and partner logos for these companies.

**How to apply:** When refreshing client letterheads, inspect rendered site branding, confirm the actual corporate mark rather than relying on the first logo or image-search result, and download it from the company's own public website/media host. Keep the original artwork unchanged.
